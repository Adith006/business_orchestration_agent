
# Business Orchestrator Agent
### Autonomous AI Workforce & Business Orchestration Platform

**One CEO. Unlimited Possibilities. An Entire AI Workforce.**

NEXORA is an AI-powered business orchestration platform where users define business goals and a central AI CEO plans, coordinates, and delegates work to specialized AI employees.

## Core Capabilities

- AI CEO for business planning and orchestration.
- Dynamic creation and management of specialist AI employees.
- Multi-agent collaboration and task delegation.
- Organizational memory and knowledge retrieval.
- MCP-based tool and capability integration.
- Human approvals, security, and governance.
- Task monitoring and business deliverable generation.
- Infrastructure and resource management.

## Technology Direction

- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI, Pydantic
- **AI Orchestration:** Deep Agents, LangGraph
- **LLM Providers:** OpenAI, xAI Grok
- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript
- **Data:** PostgreSQL, Qdrant, Redis
- **Infrastructure:** Docker, Terraform
- **CI/CD:** GitHub Actions
